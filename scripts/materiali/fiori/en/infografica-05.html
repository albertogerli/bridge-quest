Playing with Trumps

One suit gets special power: a player out of the suit led can win the trick with a trump.

1

Trumps, ruffs and fits

To ruff: to win a trick with a trump when you have no cards left in the suit led.
Fit: your side holds at least 8 cards in a suit. A trump contract pays when your side holds more trumps than the opponents.
Even a weak suit pays as trumps: with ♥ QJ10987 you take at least 4 tricks.
In the trump suit, cards sure to be established count as winners.
2

Drawing trumps

4♥ · West leads the A♠ and K♠

South ruffs the K♠. Cashing the diamonds at once, West would ruff the third round. First three rounds of hearts to draw the defenders' trumps, then the diamonds safely: 12 tricks, 6 hearts, 4 diamonds, 2 clubs. In notrump South would have lost the first five.

Side-suit winners are only relative: cash them safely only after drawing trumps.

Control: by ruffing, South stopped West's spades. No trick was gained: 5 hearts cashed plus 1 ruff still make 6.

3

Ruffing in the short hand

4♠ · West leads the K♣

Winners: 6 spades, 1 heart, 2 diamonds, 1 club. But spades can produce 8 tricks instead of 6, ruffing with dummy's trumps:

  1. A♣, A♥ and a heart ruffed in dummy.
  2. A spade to a high honor in hand, then a heart ruffed in dummy.
  3. Back to hand with the K♦, draw trumps, A♦: 12 tricks.
Don't always draw trumps at once: first take the ruffs in the short hand, the one with fewer trumps.

A ruff gains only if it adds at least one trick to what the suit would give anyway.

4

Establishing with ruffs

NORTH
SOUTH
4♠ · West leads the K♦

In a trump contract you can establish a long suit without losing the lead: the low cards you would have to lose get ruffed instead.

  1. A♦, two rounds of trumps.
  2. K♣, A♣ and a club ruffed in hand: if the opponents' clubs split 3-2, they all fall.
  3. Dummy's three clubs are good, with the ace of hearts as the entry: 13 tricks.
