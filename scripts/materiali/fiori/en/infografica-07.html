5

Which suit to open

1♥ and 1♠ need at least five cards; 1♦ at least four; otherwise 1♣, even with two.

With one long suit, open it; with two suits of five or more, open the higher-ranking.

two five-card suits: the higher1♦
no five-card major, only two diamonds1♣
long suit1♣
6

Strong openings

2♦, 2♥, 2♠: an unbalanced hand with 21+ HCP, or with about 8–9 winners. Open your long suit.

2NT: balanced, 21–23. 2♣: balanced with 24+, or a strong club-based hand.

Responder may not pass a two-level suit opening.
7

Preemptive openings

At the 3 and 4 levels: a suit of at least 7 cards with at least two honors, and nothing outside. The message: "on my own I take 3 tricks fewer than I bid."
