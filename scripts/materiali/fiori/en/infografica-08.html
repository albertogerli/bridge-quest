Opening and Response

1NT and 2NT describe the hand almost completely: responder adds up the points and picks the contract.

1

The 1NT opening

15, 16 or 17 HCP and a balanced hand (4-3-3-3, 4-4-3-2, 5-3-3-2): open 1NT, which takes priority over a suit opening.

It promises at least two cards in every suit.
