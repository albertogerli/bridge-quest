Opening bids of 1 in a suit. The responses

Responder looks for a fit and measures strength: each response tells opener to stop or go on.

1

Three kinds of response

Limit bids

They show a minimum and a maximum: opener may pass.

Invitational

They show a limit but ask opener for something extra.

Forcing

A new suit: opener may not pass.

With 5 or more points never pass: opener may hold up to 20, and 25 between you means game.
2

Raises

ResponsePointsMeaning
1♦ – 2♦5-9limit: with a minimum opener, no game
1♥ – 3♥10-11invitational: game if opener has 14+
1♠ – 4♠max 114+ card fit and a hand made for ruffing

Over 1♥/1♠ three-card support is enough; over 1♦ you need four. Raises are not forcing.

PARTNER OPENS 1♦
→ 2♦: 7 points and four diamonds.
3

Revaluing with a fit

With a 4+ card fit and an unbalanced hand, responder adds to the HCP: number of trumps minus cards in the shortest suit.
PARTNER OPENS 1♥
9 HCP + (4 − 1) = 12 → 4♥.

Not with 4-3-3-3: without a short suit there is nothing to ruff.

4

New suits: forcing

ResponsePointsMeaning
1♣ – 1♥5+one-over-one: 4+ cards, no upper limit; opener must bid again
1♠ – 2♦12+two-over-one: forcing to game

Opening 1♥ or 1♠ shows five; as a response, four are enough.

PARTNER OPENS 1♣
→ 1♥: shows four hearts. Partner raises, bids 1♠ or rebids.
  • Two five-card suits: the higher-ranking first.
  • A five-card and a four-card suit: the five-card suit first.
  • Two four-card suits: the cheaper first.
Don't jump if you can bid your suit at the 1 level: 1♥ shows 5 or more points, even 17.
5

Notrump responses

ResponsePointsMeaning
1NT5-10not forcing; denies a 4-card suit biddable at the 1 level
2NT11invitational; denies a major-suit fit
3NT12-15game: balanced, no playable major
PARTNER OPENS 1♥
Two five-card suits, only 8 points: too weak for the 2 level → 1NT.
6

Before you respond: a checklist

1 · Do I have a fit?Show it now, at the level my points allow.
2 · If not, a new suit?Bid it: it is forcing and has no upper limit.
3 · Neither?Respond in notrump, according to my points.
