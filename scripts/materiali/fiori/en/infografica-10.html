Beginner course

The opener describes

With the second bid opener tells how strong the hand is and how it is shaped: partner needs two bids to know.

1

Decide or describe?

Non-forcing response

Opener decides: pass, invite or bid game.

One-over-one response

Opener describes strength and shape.

Two-over-one response

Opener describes shape only: game is already assured.

2

Minimum and strong hands

Minimum · 12-15Describe the hand without passing the safety level.
Strong · 16-20Go past the safety level to show the extra strength.
Safety level: the last contract beyond which, with no fit and only 18-19 points between you, a reasonable result is no longer sure.
3

Minimum hand after a one-over-one

BalancedSafety level = 1NT
UnbalancedSafety level = 2 of the opening suit
1♥ – 1♠ – ?
Unbalanced, safety level 2♥: diamonds fit below it → 2♦.
1♣ – 1♠ – ?
Balanced, safety level 1NT: hearts would go past it → 1NT.
4

Strong hand: go past the safety level

After a one-over-one response, with a strong hand opener always describes it by going past the safety level.
1♣ – 1♦ – ?
Spades with a jump → 2♠: at least 16 points, four spades and long clubs.
1♥ – 1♠ – ?
Within the safety level it would be 2♣; the jump → 3♣ shows the strength.
1♦ – 1♠ – ?
16 points and four spades: raise to the 3 level → 3♠.
BALANCED 18-20 Too much for 1NT, too little to open 2NT: jump to 2NT (1♥ – 1♠ – 2NT).
5

After a two-over-one

The response is forcing to game: opener describes, whether minimum or strong.

WEST 1♦ – EAST 2♣ – WEST ?
Nothing extra, but opener describes → 2♥. East, no longer worried about that suit, bids 3NT.
6

Then responder

After a one-over-one, once opener has bid twice:

1NT, old suit at minimum levelnot forcing
2NT, old suit with a jumpinvitational
new suitforcing

A new suit is the only way to make opener bid again: it acts as a prod and needs no length.
