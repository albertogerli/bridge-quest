The Overcall

How to enter the opponents' auction: three ways, each with its own requirements.

1

Three ways to compete

An overcall is the first call other than Pass by the side opposite the opener. It is a free choice: you come in only when it pays.

Double

Short in the opponent's suit, the other three suits playable. 12–16 points.

1NT

Like a 1NT opening: 15–17 balanced, with a stopper in opener's suit.

Suit overcall

A very good suit, or plenty of points that fit neither the double nor 1NT. 16/17 at most.

2

The takeout double

The double asks partner to choose a suit and bid it at the level partner thinks you can play.
over 1♣ or 1♦both majors: 4-4 or 4-3, never 4-2
over 1♥promises 4 spades
over 1♠promises 4 hearts
South opens 1♦, West to bid
Double: both four-card majors, a singleton diamond and the right strength.
South opens 1♣, your call
Pass: the points are there, but hearts are missing. Partner would feel betrayed.
3

The doubler's partner

Advancer must pick a suit: the minimum for bidding at the cheapest level is zero points.
1♦ – Double – Pass – ?
1♥: never pass. 1♦ doubled would be a disaster.
1♦ – Double – Pass – ?
4♠: bid as if you could see partner's cards, at least 4-3 in the majors.
1♦ – Double – Pass – ?
3NT: enough points, no major fit, a diamond stopper.
4

The 1NT overcall

Same strength and shape as a 1NT opening. The opponents already have a suit to lead, so you need a stopper in opener's suit.

South opens 1♥, West to bid
1NT: 17 points, balanced, a heart stopper.
5

The suit overcall

At the one level: 8–16 points, 5 cards with at least one honor (rarely 4 with two honors), or any 6 cards.

South opens 1♦
1♥: 8 points, all of them in the suit.
6

Suit overcall, two level

10–16 points, usually 6 cards with at least one honor. Exception: over 1♠, five hearts are enough, with an honor and 13+ points.

South opens 1♠
2♦: six cards with honors, 10 points.

Above 16/17 points don't overcall: double first, then bid your suit.

7

The overcaller's partner

Advancer starts from the guaranteed minimum. With 9+ points never pass: 16 + 9 = 25, game is possible. You may raise with only three cards.

Before you bid a suit, remember partner will lead it. If that makes your hair stand on end, the overcall is wrong.

In competitive auctions, 70% of the time opener's side buys the contract: every overcall guides the defense.
