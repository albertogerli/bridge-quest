Winners and Establishable Cards

As soon as dummy comes down, declarer asks: how many tricks do I have? Where can I find more?

1

Winner

A card that, played now, wins the trick. Also called a sure trick.

2 winnersthe ace and the king
2

Establishable card

A card that will become a winner after some work. Here the ace is missing: keep leading the suit until an opponent takes it.

0 winners3 establishable
3

Establishing by force

Lead the suit one card at a time until the opponents take their higher honors. It is the only technique that always works: one hundred percent of the time.

4

Equal cards

Touching cards have the same power: in AKQJ the jack wins just like the ace, 4 winners. It works across both hands too: with AQ2 in dummy, South's king is a winner.

Declarer's plan, after the opening lead

1 · Count your winners

Suit by suit, across both hands.

2 · Find what to establish

Start with the suit that can give the most tricks.

3 · Establish, then cash

Don't rush to play your winners: you need them later.

Careful: sometimes, once the suit is established, you must regain the lead before you can cash it.
